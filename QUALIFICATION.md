# On-chain RGB SDK qualification register

The candidate is an on-chain SDK review branch for Node and Android Bare. It remains private while the release gates below are open. It admits isolated Regtest for Bitcoin and RGB, plus separate Bitcoin-only and staging RGB profiles for public Signet.

Alpha.12 pins BFA.7 at `9ec49c08be2e68f08521281e3efbd760ec989cb9`. It uses a fresh BFA.7 wallet directory. BFA.7 retains the BFA.6 schema and transaction-bound lock format, and adds witness receive and prepared sweep support upstream. Hosted Dev admission remains closed while the deployment and asset export are qualified against this version. The existing alpha.10 Dev APK uses BFA.5. Dev deployed BFA.6 connector `0.1.0-207` with new asset `rgb:A6k89KtR-e3oXqRP-uH3jUrz-LU_sYL3-hMZ0k3O-6u8DXbE` on November 6. The reviewed full E2E run `42261217049` failed at mint confirmation, and no issuer-approved export or SHA-256 for the new asset was available during the November 7 review. DevOps PR #218 aligned wallet readiness to BFA.6 at `5010eefa355beb9c42a4c1b69883f7651241a111`. Hosted admission requires both the new export and successful qualification against the deployment. [Deployment](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/devops/pull/217) · [Readiness revision](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/devops/pull/218) · [E2E](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/bridge/actions/runs/42261217049)

The locked SDK uses Tether base wallet `1.0.0-beta.25`, Bare Kit `3.0.1` and Bare `1.36.2`. The [native build runbook](native/README.md) records the source trees, patches and endpoint checks.

## SDK and Iris contract

| Area | Candidate SDK surface | Iris integration and remaining qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet lifecycle | Explicit offline open and connect, awaited shutdown, account-path support, read-only WDK policy account, stable session address and explicit rotation | Iris binds approval to the authenticated wallet generation and owns awaited shutdown. Local Android lock, unlock and cold restart are exercised; iOS and physical devices remain. |
| BTC and RGB | Exact base-unit amounts, BTC receipts, blind and witness invoices, decoded invoice and transfer status, assets, balances, refresh outcomes and configured Electrum and proxy checks | Iris's on-chain adapter and exact-unit mapping are integrated. Funded local Android mint, BTC send and BFA round trip pass. Hosted services remain separately gated. |
| RGB output setup and spends | Prepare, commit, cancel and explicit pre-Begin and post-submission recovery with the original operation ID | Iris integrates reviewed output setup and operation recovery. Local cancellation, background interruption and re-preparation checks pass. Ambiguous outcomes still require reconciliation. |
| BIP-322 | Explicit message approval, owned P2TR signing, P2TR/P2WPKH simple verification | Iris's approval and signing flow passes on the local Android Release app. Physical-device authentication and iOS remain. |
| WebRGB | Request-scoped facade, explicit V1 payout route and asset allowlists, durable invoice and burn identity, proof scoping and recovery | Iris integrates persistent grants and authenticated local dispatch. Real transport cold resumption, consent rejection and delayed proof retrieval pass locally. Funded acceptance covers direct Arbitrum V1 only; hosted payout and other routes remain unqualified. |
| VSS | Startup and on-chain operations have no VSS dependency | Wallet access and on-chain operations do not depend on VSS. External-signer backup and recovery remain unqualified. |

The legacy package and the new engine have different public contracts and can derive different wallet identities from the same recovery phrase. Use distinct storage for qualification. A future public release needs an explicit breaking-version or distribution decision before replacing the published package.

## Upstream and service dependencies

1. **Hosted assets and protocol.** The existing public Signet staging USDT export uses an earlier BFA schema. Its Genesis commits to that schema, so re-encoding its bytes cannot make it compatible with the schema in this SDK. The upgraded staging profile needs an official export for a new issuance with a new asset ID, together with the exact asset metadata and export SHA-256. Verify the deployed gateway, signer, RGB proxy and Arbitrum contract as one versioned system. The contract must expose finalized mint lock records through `getFundsInBlock`. The candidate retains standalone contract registration. BFA.7 enforces mint conservation and uses a separate lock identifier tied to the mint transaction. [RGB contract import](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/rgb-lib-bfa/pull/4) · [BFA.7 mint conservation](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/rgb-lib-bfa/blob/9ec49c08be2e68f08521281e3efbd760ec989cb9/src/wallet/online.rs#L3202-L3217) · [Gateway migration](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/bridge-utexo/pull/531) · [Signer migration](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/enclave-signer/pull/337) · [Bridge contracts](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/bridge-smart-contracts/pull/185)
2. **Public Signet staging.** Both Signet profiles pin `ssl://electrum.iriswallet.com:50033` with certificate validation and verify genesis and the public Signet block-one hash. Staging RGB also pins `rpcs://staging.rgb-proxy.utexo.com/json-rpc` and `https://arb1.arbitrum.io/rpc`. The native egress overlay checks DNS at connection time and disallows redirects and system proxies for RGB proxy, reject-list and EVM RPC requests. Iris keeps the staging asset and bridge admission closed pending a compatible asset export and a matching deployment. Funded local fixture results cover the local codec and proof recovery. Hosted payout needs its own funded test. [RGB SDK endpoints](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/rgb-sdk-rn#rgb_l1-connection)
3. **VSS and RGB restore.** Explicit BTC-only restore discovers same-engine Bitcoin within the HD scan gap and keeps RGB mutations disabled in that directory. RGB ownership needs matching client-side state. External-signer VSS needs consistent snapshot capture and acknowledgement on the current RGB graph, authenticated storage, and a restore path that validates the wallet identity and RGB state before admitting mutations. The SDK starts and transacts independently of VSS. [Snapshot proposal](https://github.com/UTEXO-Protocol/rgb-lib-bfa/blob/abd41d3afff6d8fb11461e26e843301650e22df3/docs/contract-registration-and-snapshots.md)
4. **Stock Tether lifecycle and Expo support.** WDK Core `dispose()` and Pear's default reset do not await async teardown. A host-owned path can await explicit SDK shutdown. Stock reset needs an upstream lifecycle contract before reuse. The current React Native core declares `expo-crypto >=55 <57`, outside Iris's Expo 57 graph. [WDK Core](https://github.com/tetherto/wdk/blob/c8a252fa127d9fc4a580252d3e46b4eaf372315a/src/wdk.js#L331-L342) · [Pear reset](https://github.com/tetherto/pear-wrk-wdk/blob/953af3cf7bf4ada33cc66e63185d1bdcf70d427b/src/handlers/lifecycle.js#L248-L268) · [React Native core peers](https://github.com/tetherto/wdk-react-native-core/blob/30fc571d00b19839476c32a919356087711c5c7d/package.json)
5. **Dependency maintenance.** The locked Rust graph has an unmaintained `unsafe-libyaml` dependency via `rgb-strict-types` and `serde_yaml`. Resolve it through a compatible upstream graph or a separately reviewed patch. The local audit reported zero known vulnerabilities in the locked graph.

## Remaining SDK, app and qualification work

- **Incident handling.** Native journal writes share the wallet directory lock. Legacy ambiguous records, orphaned unsigned preparation files and a submitted operation with insufficient evidence to resolve its fate still require an operator procedure. Never edit the journal to bypass an unresolved spend.
- **Product worklet and transport.** Iris's on-chain adapter, authenticated approval, awaited shutdown and separate trusted WebRGB dispatch are implemented and locally exercised. Core policy conditions get a read-only view; the typed SDK signer limits spends to reviewed native plans. Broader device, relay, network and payout-route acceptance remains separate from the recorded local transport cases.
- **Acceptance and distribution.** Qualify the product on iOS and physical devices, and perform funded Signet and hosted payouts after service prerequisites are available. Preserve the distinction between the funded Android APK and later presentation-only builds. Complete release artifact attestation, third-party notices and distribution-rights review, and the public breaking-version decision. The local build inventory records declared licenses and notice hashes, with further redistribution review for embedded dependencies and platform runtimes.
